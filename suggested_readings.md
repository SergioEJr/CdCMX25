Entertaining videos I have watched and think are great at providing conceptual and general understanding.

- [Central Limit Theorem by 3Blue1Brown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zeJD6dqJ5lo&t=1611s) - This is an excellent channel for math enthusiasts

## Physics Resources

Serious resources for deep dives into the material.

[1] [The Theoretical Minimum: Statistical Mechanics by Leonard Susskind at Stanford](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D1RzvXDXyqA&list=PL_IkS0viawhr3HcKH607rXbVqy28W_gB7) - A great set of lecture videos that lies somewhere between pop science and an actual physics course.

[2] [MIT Open Courseware Introduction to Statistical Physics](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/res-8-010-introduction-to-statistical-physics-summer-2018/pages/syllabus/) - A statistical mechanics course taught to high school students over the course of six weeks. This is a good place to start if you want to seriously learn the subject.

[3] Random Walks in Biology by Howard Berg - A great overview of random walks and how they are relevant to biology.
